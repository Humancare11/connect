What happens

  1. On every page load, on desktop and phones, the doctor walks in from the right and waves (first 3 seconds of the video), his feet on the bottom line of the message bar (the same on phones, where the box sits left of the round button).
  2. At about 3.2 s the white "Write a message…" box slides in at its final place, left of him. The arrow turns black when there is text.
  3. After the video ends he keeps standing and waving (only the part from 4.0 s loops).
  4. After 10 seconds of visible, idle time only the doctor shrinks into the round button; the box stays where it is. Typing, focusing the box or a hidden tab holds the 10 seconds.
  5. The round button shows the live avatar (red LIVE badge, teal ring that fills), then fades to black, then shows the chat icon, and repeats.
  6. Typing in the box and sending, or clicking the doctor or the round button, opens the chat. The typed text waits above the name and email form and is sent as the first message after Start chat.
  7. Closing the chat brings back the compact bar. Visitors who prefer reduced motion or have data saver on, and browsers where the video cannot start (for example iOS Low Power Mode), get the compact bar directly.

About the videos

  • doctor-alpha.webm (VP9 with alpha, 360 x 556, no sound) is the intro in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Android; it has no poster, so nothing of the doctor shows before the walk-in's first frame.
  • doctor-ios.mp4 (H.264, same framing, light-grey background) is the intro in Safari, iOS and iPadOS, with a soft mask on its edges.
  • doctor.webm / doctor.mp4 (grey studio background) are only used inside the round avatar, cropped to head and shoulders.
  • All files are loaded with ?v=1; bump it when a file is replaced.
