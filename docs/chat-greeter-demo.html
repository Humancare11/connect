What happens

  1. The doctor walks in and waves (first 3 seconds of your video).
  2. A white “Write a message…” box opens beside him, with a round arrow button. The patient types there; the arrow turns black when there is text.
  3. After the video ends he keeps standing and waving (the last part loops).
  4. The patient types and sends. The chat opens with the name and email form, and the typed message is sent once the chat starts.
  5. Clicking the doctor opens the chat directly.
  6. After 10 seconds the doctor shrinks into a small round avatar next to the message box. The avatar keeps playing his video with a red “LIVE” badge and a teal ring that fills up. If the patient is typing, it waits.
  7. When the ring is full, the avatar fades to black and turns into the chat icon. After a few seconds it switches back to the live avatar, and the two keep alternating.
  8. Typing in the white box and sending, or clicking the round button (avatar or chat icon), opens the chat. Closing the chat brings the compact bar back. On phones only the round button shows.
