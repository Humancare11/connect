Preview notes: rows are made up. Auto entries are bookings made on the website or app: you fill in the green columns and they save as you go. Manual entries are cases added by hand (for example from a partner company); only that view has the Add manual entry button, and it shows every field in the same columns and order as your Excel sheet (scroll sideways; Date, Company, Case Number and Patient Name stay pinned on the left, Actions on the right). Edit turns that row into editable cells right in the table (Save or Cancel in Actions, Enter saves, Escape cancels); once "Payment to provider" is Paid, the case, provider, patient and fee cells lock. History shows who changed what and when. The three money boxes on top show only for Super Admin: switch the preview to Admin to see them disappear.
